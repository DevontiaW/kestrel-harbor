Where should a person decide?

A shop owner asks to sign off every refund the AI (artificial intelligence) assistant approves. It feels safe. Within a week, refunds wait two days for the owner, customers complain, and the owner is doing nothing but signing.

A human approval step is a station with limited time, like any other. How often it fires decides whether it is a safety check or the new bottleneck.

On the agent floor, a shift lead approves every {{approveNth}} task, and each approval takes {{approveMinutes}} minutes. Two reviewers keep review moving, so approval is the only human decision left to study.

Build it yourself with AI

Optional. This prompt adds an approval rule you can change. It is a starter for learning, not a workflow design for a business. Then check its work against the numbers below.

The one rule

Approval minutes needed per hour = items per hour that need approval × minutes each. If that is more than the person's spare minutes, approval becomes the bottleneck.

Here: approving every task needs {{approveAll.load}} minutes an hour from one person who has 60.
