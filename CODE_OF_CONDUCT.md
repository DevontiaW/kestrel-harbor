# Code of conduct

This is a learning space. People arrive with different backgrounds, and many are not engineers.

## Expected

- Be patient and kind, especially with beginner questions.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Credit other people's work.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity or background.
- Sharing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Reporting

Report a problem through the repository's private reporting channel, or open an issue asking a maintainer to contact you privately. Maintainers will respond, keep reports confidential, and may remove content or block participants who break these rules.
